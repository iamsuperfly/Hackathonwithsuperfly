**Key Impacts in Scope (Critical)**:
- Direct theft of user funds
- Permanent freezing of funds
- Protocol insolvency
- Invalid oracle price updates

**High Impacts**:
- Bypassing timelocks or fee caps
- Unauthorized role usage
- Theft of unclaimed yield/royalties

**Out of Scope**:
- Issues requiring malicious trusted roles (e.g., Factory Owner, Oracle ADMIN_ROLE)
- Known issues from audits
- UI/frontend bugs (unless primacy of impact)
